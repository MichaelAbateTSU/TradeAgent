## Statistical-gate audit

The audit confirmed four material defects in v0.9:

1. Candidate PBO compared one candidate with its benchmark. PBO must rank the in-sample
   winner across competing configurations, so the candidate values were not valid
   family-level statistics.
2. Family PBO used five fold totals split into four blocks of sizes 1/1/1/2. CSCV requires
   equal contiguous blocks.
3. DSR used five fold-total returns and assumed Sharpe dispersion from sample length. It
   omitted daily cash/zero-exposure observations and the cross-trial Sharpe dispersion
   required by DSR.
4. Alpaca daily bars were labeled at midnight even though their OHLC values were only
   known at the session close. That caused look-ahead timing and initially produced zero
   SIP coverage because evidence requests occurred while the market was closed.

The corrected implementation:

- forms synchronous daily portfolio-return panels, including explicit zero-exposure days;
- aligns candidate and benchmark daily returns before Sharpe and DSR;
- partitions time into equal contiguous CSCV blocks;
- enumerates every half-block combination;
- selects the highest in-sample Sharpe and ranks that same configuration out of sample;
- uses ascending OOS rank, `rank / (N + 1)`, average tie ranks, and
  `log(rank / (1 - rank))`;
- counts non-positive logits as overfit;
- retains all 30 genuinely attempted lower-turnover configurations;
- deduplicates deterministic reruns as hypotheses;
- estimates effective trials as `1 + (M - 1) * (1 - average correlation)`;
- uses the observed cross-trial periodic-Sharpe dispersion in DSR; and
- keeps annualization at 252 only for displayed daily metrics.

These choices follow Bailey et al.,
[The Probability of Backtest Overfitting](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf),
and Bailey and López de Prado,
[The Deflated Sharpe Ratio](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf).

Deterministic tests now produce PBO exactly 0 for persistent ordering, 0.5 for mixed OOS
ranks, and 1 for perfect regime reversal. Additional tests cover equal-block rejection,
rank/logit direction, duplicate reruns, correlated effective trials, daily zero returns,
and benchmark/date alignment.

## Lower-turnover execution calibration

Alpaca SIP snapshots were requested for every trading date and symbol, which is a strict
superset of every entry, exit, decision, retry, and benchmark adjustment generated by all
30 configurations.

| Item | Result |
| --- | ---: |
| Development trades represented | 10,628 |
| Unique SIP anchors | 26,418 |
| Trading dates | 1,258 |
| Two-sided quote coverage | 99.996% |
| Two-sided trade coverage | 99.568% |
| Market-order missed fills | 0 |
| Market-order partial fills across all alternative configurations | 7,229 |
| Decision-price marketable-limit misses | 17,082 |

Sparse windows were retried up to 60 seconds. One XLV benchmark-only close lacked a quote
after the close; the last quote before the close was used conservatively. Missing evidence
never received a fabricated price.

The market model crosses observed bid/ask, adds the greater of 0.5 bps or adverse
submission-window quote movement, caps quantity at displayed size, and records unfilled
quantity. The marketable-limit model fixes its limit at the decision-time opposite quote;
an OHLC touch never creates a fill.

Regulatory fees use:

- SEC Section 31: $20.60 per $1 million sold;
- FINRA TAF: $0.000195 per share sold, capped at $9.79 per trade; and
- CAT: $0.000003 per share bought or sold.

Each fee type is rounded up to one cent at end of day. Costs were rerun at 1×, 1.5×, 2×,
and 3×. Across the 30 alternative configurations, the analytical sum was $2,371.99 spread,
$1,707.43 conservative slippage, and $811.61 regulatory fees. These sums describe
alternative backtests and are not one tradable portfolio.

Twenty-nine configurations remained positive in absolute terms, but **zero of 30 beat
their equal-exposure benchmark after observed costs**. The apparent edge was market
exposure, not demonstrated alpha. Corrected DSR stayed below 0.47 for the selected plateau
members, and observed-cost PBO was 0.988 and 0.976 for the two surviving families. Swing
mean reversion was retired before external testing.
